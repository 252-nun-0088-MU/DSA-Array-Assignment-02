Initially, the stack is empty.

1. Push(10)

We insert 10 into the empty stack.

Stack: [10]

2. Push(20)

Now we insert 20. It is placed on top of 10.

Stack: [10, 20]

3. Push(30)

We insert 30 on top of 20.

Stack: [10, 20, 30]

4. Pop()

The pop operation removes the top element of the stack. In this case, 30 is removed.

Stack: [10, 20]

5. Push(40)

Now we insert 40 into the stack. It becomes the new top element.

Stack: [10, 20, 40]

6. Pop()

Again, we remove the top element. This time, 40 is removed from the stack.

Stack: [10, 20]
