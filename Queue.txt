Initially, the queue is empty.

1. Enqueue(10)

We insert 10 into the empty queue.

Queue: [10]

2. Enqueue(20)

Now we insert 20 at the end of the queue.

Queue: [10, 20]

3. Enqueue(30)

We insert 30 at the end of the queue.

Queue: [10, 20, 30]

4. Dequeue()

The dequeue operation removes the first element of the queue. In this case, 10 is removed.

Queue: [20, 30]

5. Enqueue(40)

Now we insert 40 at the end of the queue.

Queue: [20, 30, 40]

6. Dequeue()

Again, we remove the first element. This time, 20 is removed.

Queue: [30, 40]

Final Result
After performing all the given operations, the final queue contains:

[30, 40]

Front element: 30

Rear element: 40

Total elements: 2
